This is the Test

Nietzsche said that all truly great thoughts are conceived while walking. I am not sure about great, but I am certain about most of mine.

There is something about the rhythm of it. The body occupied just enough to quiet the restless part of the mind, leaving the rest free to wander. Problems that felt stuck at the desk loosen after a mile. Sentences that refused to come arrive whole.

No destination

The trick, I think, is to walk without purpose. Not to the store, not for exercise, not while listening to something. Just walking, with nothing to consume and nowhere to be.

It feels unproductive, which is exactly why it works. The mind needs slack to make connections, and a walk is one of the few places left where we still allow ourselves slack.

Solvitur ambulando. It is solved by walking.

Tomorrow, try leaving your phone at home for twenty minutes. See what you think about.
