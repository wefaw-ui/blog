Solivarii is a small publication about attention, craft, and the quiet work of making things. It is written slowly, published irregularly, and kept deliberately plain.

There are no ads, no trackers, and no algorithm deciding what you see. Just essays, in order, for as long as there is something worth saying.

## Who writes it

Solivarii is written by one person who builds software for a living and thinks about it far too much in their spare time. Previous lives include design, teaching, and a brief, ill-advised stint in restaurant kitchens.

The essays here are working notes more than finished conclusions. Disagreement is welcome. The best way to reach me is on [X](https://x.com/solivarii).

## Principles

- **Plain over clever.** Every page should load instantly and read clearly on any screen.
- **Slow over frequent.** A post goes up when it is ready, not when a schedule says so.
- **Independent.** Solivarii is funded by readers, not advertisers.
